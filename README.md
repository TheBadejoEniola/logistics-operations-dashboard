## logistics-operations-dashboard
---
## Project Overview

This project is my TS Academy capstone project, built to analyze logistics operations and provide insights into areas such as driver performance, route profitability, fleet utilization, fuel usage, maintenance, and safety.
The project uses a relational dataset containing 14 tables covering drivers, trucks, trailers, customers, routes, loads, trips, fuel, maintenance, safety incidents, and other operational data.

## Skills Demonstrated

* Data cleaning and transformation
* Data modeling and relationships
* DAX calculations
* Dashboard design and visualization
* KPI development
* Data analysis and interpretation
* Interactive reporting
  
## Tools Used

* Power BI
* Power Query
* DAX

## Dashboard Review

The dashboard provides insights into different areas of logistics operations, including:

* Driver Performance
* Route Profitability
* Fleet Utilization
* Maintenance
* Fuel Analysis
* Safety Incidents
* Overall Operations

Interactive visuals and filters allow users to explore the data and identify trends and areas that may require attention.

## Key Insights

- **₦262.53M total revenue** generated with **₦225.09M in profit**, resulting in an **86% profit margin**.
- **56% on-time delivery rate**, indicating room for improvement in delivery performance.
- **184K total miles** covered with an average fuel efficiency of **6.48 MPG**.
- **₦37.44M total fuel cost** across approximately **28.42K gallons** consumed.
- **₦5.73M total maintenance cost**, with maintenance costing approximately **₦31.10 per mile**.
- **170 safety incidents** recorded across the fleet, highlighting the need for continued safety monitoring.
- **72.23K total downtime** recorded, which may affect fleet availability and delivery efficiency.
- Customer and route performance varied significantly, highlighting opportunities to focus on high-performing customers and more profitable routes.
- Driver performance varied across revenue, mileage, fuel efficiency, and on-time delivery, providing opportunities to identify both strong performers and areas for improvement.
